Privacy & Confidentiality

All information shared in therapy is confidential and protected by law. This page summarizes data handling for The Safe Space Studio. For full details, review or request the complete policy.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information (name, email, phone)
  • Session notes and clinical records (secure storage)
  • Payment information processed through secure providers

How We Use Your Information

To provide therapy, schedule appointments, process payments, and comply with legal obligations.

Exceptions to Confidentiality

Confidentiality may be broken for imminent risk of harm, abuse, or court order.
